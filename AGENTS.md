# Enemy visual standards

When adding or reworking campaign enemies:

- Give each species an intentional, recognizable silhouette and anatomy. Do not reuse an unrelated creature model with a new color.
- Preserve successful simple designs such as Moss Slime; low detail alone is not a reason to redesign an enemy.
- Prefer simple, readable shapes and a few identifying details over visual clutter. Normal enemies should stay lightweight; bosses need a distinct focal feature.
- Match materials and colors to the creature and its region. Preserve the game's block-style appearance.
- Give different bosses distinct designs rather than repeating the same body with new decorations.
- Check the model in the actual browser renderer and almanac preview, including framing, attack poses, red hit flashes, and reduced motion. Check finite geometry and restore drawing transforms after rendering.
- Preserve enemy identities, attacks, stats and saved-run compatibility unless gameplay changes were requested.

The user prefers the restored larger UI. Do not reintroduce the compact landscape layout or the removed Expedition Rally/Banner Arena modes without a request.

The title menu should use the original single vertical list down the left side. Do not rearrange menu actions into right-hand columns without a request.
